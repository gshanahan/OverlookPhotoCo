About

My name is Gabriel Shanahan and I'm an amature nature photographer living in East Tennessee. My preferred medium is 35mm film because it helps me focus more on the process, and less on the outcome (You also can't beat the grain and personality of good film stock). I'm not on social media, but wanted a centralized repository to share my pictures and document my thoughts. Hopefully some can find enjoyment in the work, and suggest new areas for me to explore.

The idea behind Overlook Photography is to document things we often overlook during our day-to-day lives, continue to grow as an amature nature photographer, and document my process along the way.
